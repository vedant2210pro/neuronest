Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from parents and students about the NeuroNest platform.

Is NeuroNest a coaching class?

+

No, we are not a coaching class. Unlike classes, we do not teach subjects like Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, or Biology. Students already attend school and coaching institutes to learn concepts. Our role is strictly to help students manage routine schedules, maintain consistency, resolve backlogs, track daily work, and build study discipline.

Who is it for?

+

NeuroNest is for JEE, NEET, and MHT-CET aspirants who have academic study materials but struggle with backlogs, irregular study hours, low self-discipline, time management, or exam anxiety.

How does the accountability system work?

+

Students use their digital tracker to submit a short daily check-in report. Mentors monitor this data daily. If a student misses a check-in or falls behind on goals, the mentor immediately follows up via WhatsApp or calls to address the issue.

Do you teach academic subjects?

+

No. We focus strictly on study strategies, mindset coaching, goal setting, routine tracking, and well-being. We help you make the best use of resources you already have from school and coaching classes.

How are mentorship sessions conducted?

+

Mentorship sessions are conducted live over Google Meet. They consist of one-on-one checks to build schedules, review analytics, and troubleshoot specific habits, alongside occasional group sessions focusing on study methodology.

What is the NeuroNest Tracker?

+

It is our customized digital dashboard where students log daily goals, track habits, record mood levels, log study hours, and list active backlogs. Both the student and the mentor have live access to this dashboard to plan routes scientifically.
